# Full-corpus editorial integrity policy

Date: 2026-10-02

## Why this exists

A public Culture Check verdict must not depend on the site owner noticing a bad classification after publication. Owner feedback is a calibration input, not the quality-control mechanism.

The Sita Ramam review exposed a structural issue: older v0.x/v1.x records could still present durable verdicts even though they predated the evidence-derived v2 dossier, the symmetric community-contempt screen, the sacred/religious-valence probe and the current red-team requirement.

## Publication invariant

A durable public verdict must be backed by the evidence-derived v2 methodology.

Legacy records remain available for revision history and migration, but they do not auto-publish until revalidated. Existing legacy owner approvals do not bypass this migration requirement.

## Required checks for every migrated title

1. Film understanding before verdicting: plot, protagonist/antagonist, ending and decisive moral actions.
2. Rashtra / Raksha / Dharma / Itihasa / Parampara / Local Roots / Social Dharma / Sacred Regard scored from the actual narrative, not genre or reputation.
3. Symmetric community-contempt screen across caste, religious, regional and linguistic groups.
4. Sacred/religious-valence screen for deity, scripture, temple, ritual, priestly and devotional treatment.
5. Distinguish criticism of a person, practice or institution from generalized contempt toward a community or tradition.
6. Source/adaptation and true-story checks where applicable.
7. Fact / interpretation / intent separated explicitly.
8. Strongest counter-reading researched and retained.
9. Material ambiguity escalated instead of silently auto-publishing.
10. Narrative-integrity caveats remain separate from cultural verdict unless they materially change the cultural meaning.

## Migration order

The entire live corpus is in scope. Highest priority is:

- legacy Certified / Mixed / Not Certified records;
- films involving religion, caste, community identity, Kashmir, military/national-service themes, historical figures, sacred adaptations or ideological controversy;
- legacy records with null dimensions, generic notes or sparse evidence;
- remaining legacy Neutral records.

Hardened v2 records remain public but are still subject to challenge and re-audit when new evidence appears.

## Regression rule

No title may remain public merely because it was previously published. If a v2 re-audit changes the verdict, the corrected verdict takes precedence and the older record remains visible only in revision history.
