# Sanghi Certified — Master Plan v0.2

**Project:** FlickPick  
**Status:** Pilot methodology, supersedes v0.1  
**Purpose:** Define an India-grounded, evidence-backed editorial certification layer for Indian cinema.

## 1. Product thesis

FlickPick solves the "endless scroll" problem by narrowing a streaming pool and shuffling to a single recommendation. Sanghi Certified adds a second question:

> Before I invest my time, how does this film treat India, Indic civilization, dharma, inherited traditions, history, national identity, sovereignty and culturally sensitive representation?

This is **not a movie-quality rating** and is **not a generic Western Left/Right classifier**. It is an explicitly Bharatiya/India-grounded editorial lens whose rules and evidence are published so users can inspect and challenge individual findings.

The product remains **FlickPick**. **Sanghi Certified** is a badge/mode inside FlickPick.

## 2. Declared sovereignty baseline

Sanghi Certified evaluates Indian national-security and territorial-integrity narratives from the constitutional and stated Government of India position.

For certification purposes:

- India is treated as a sovereign constitutional republic whose unity and territorial integrity are positive baseline values.
- Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh are evaluated from India's constitutional/state position that they are integral parts of India.
- A film is **not penalized** merely because it presents an Indian sovereignty/integration perspective without giving equal narrative weight to Pakistan's competing claim.
- External territorial claims do not reduce a film's Rashtra/Raksha alignment simply because they exist.
- The integrity audit still checks concrete factual propositions inside the chosen frame: dates, persons, operations, quoted statements, chronology, fabricated incidents, identity substitutions, etc.
- Therefore **"India's side of the story" and "factual accuracy" are separate questions**. A film can strongly align with Indian sovereignty while still containing checkable historical inaccuracies, and vice versa.

Reference baseline: Government of India and Indian constitutional/judicial materials consistently state that Jammu & Kashmir is an integral part of India; the methodology adopts that position as an editorial premise rather than treating the certification layer as a neutral arbitration of competing territorial claims.

## 3. What certification is — and is not

### It is

- A worldview/discovery signal.
- India-first and region-aware.
- Evidence-backed and explainable.
- Capable of certifying films for different reasons: Dharma, Rashtra, Itihasa, Parampara, Raksha, Local Roots, etc.
- Separate from artistic quality, box office, popularity and conventional star ratings.
- Explicit about uncertainty and disputed interpretations.

### It is not

- A BJP-support score.
- A Western conservative/progressive score translated to Indian films.
- A rule that caste criticism, social reform, anti-tyranny themes or female agency are automatically disqualifying.
- A rule that the identity of one villain proves prejudice.
- A rule that Hindu characters being good or non-Hindu characters being bad determines certification.
- A claim of political neutrality.

## 4. Core dimensions

1. **Dharma / Sacred Regard** — treatment of Indic sacred ideas, deities, rituals and religious life.
2. **Civilizational Continuity** — whether the film understands contemporary India as connected to an older civilizational inheritance.
3. **Rashtra** — treatment of India, sovereignty, national cohesion and belonging.
4. **Itihasa / Historical Memory** — treatment of Indian historical memory, epics, colonialism, invasions and inherited narratives.
5. **Parampara** — treatment of family, temple, local custom, gurus, festivals, ritual and inherited practice.
6. **Local Roots** — whether regional cultural worlds are understood on their own terms rather than flattened into generic Western categories.
7. **Raksha** — treatment of armed forces, terrorism, intelligence, border security and national defence.
8. **Social Dharma** — how caste, injustice, gender, poverty and reform are handled, including whether critique occurs within or through wholesale contempt for the civilizational frame.
9. **Sacred vs Cynical** — whether faith and inherited meaning are allowed to be genuine or systematically reduced to ignorance, manipulation or superstition.
10. **Contempt / Caricature Check** — whether a community, caste, religious practice or national identity is used as shorthand for ridicule, villainy or moral inferiority.

A film need not score highly on every dimension to certify.

## 5. Narrative Integrity Audit

Certification and integrity are separate outputs.

The audit looks for:

- Adaptation Delta
- Identity Asymmetry
- Caricature
- Selective Historicity
- Religious Valence
- Narrative Agency
- Contempt Signal
- Source Fidelity

Possible integrity findings include:

- Omission
- Compression
- Composite character
- Fictional addition
- Identity substitution
- Historical inversion
- Ideological substitution

A film may be Sanghi Certified while carrying an integrity flag.

## 6. Facts / Interpretation / Intent separation

Every sensitive finding must distinguish:

### Fact
What can be independently established from the film or reliable source material.

### Interpretation
What the established fact may mean in the narrative.

### Intent
Whether evidence establishes that the filmmaker deliberately sought a particular ideological effect.

Intent requires substantially stronger evidence than a scene-level discrepancy.

Example structure:

```text
FACT: Real-life subject had identity X; film omits or changes it.
INTERPRETATION: The change creates a representation asymmetry.
INTENT: Not established / supported / strongly supported.
```

## 7. Social-media intelligence: radar, not verdict

X/Twitter, Reddit, YouTube and other public discussion are essential because small scenes, visual symbols and adaptation changes are often absent from professional reviews.

However, social engagement is **not evidence weighting**. Ten thousand reposts remain one allegation until verified.

### 7.1 Discovery query packs

For every film, generate multilingual searches combining:

- film title / character / director
- `real story`, `true story`, `changed`, `deleted`, `hidden`, `caste`, `religion`, `Brahmin`, `Iyengar`, `Dalit`, `Muslim`, `Christian`, `Hindu`, `temple`, `church`, `Periyar`, `Ambedkar`, `RSS`, `army`, `Pakistan`, `Kashmir`, `conversion`, `name changed`, `community`, `wedding`
- equivalent Tamil, Malayalam, Telugu, Kannada and Hindi vocabulary
- the real-life person's name versus the fictional character's name

The system should also search creator interviews, regional-language commentary and quote-tweets/replies where the original allegation may have originated.

### 7.2 Lead quality scoring

A social claim becomes a high-priority lead when it includes one or more of:

1. timestamp or exact scene
2. film clip or frame
3. screenshot of source material
4. named real-life person/event
5. before/after comparison
6. primary document, memoir or interview
7. a falsifiable claim rather than a generic ideological opinion

### 7.3 Claim clustering

Near-duplicate posts are collapsed into a single claim cluster. Popularity can raise investigation priority but never confidence in truth.

## 8. Hidden-narrative discovery pipeline

The pipeline for catching one-off scenes and subtle adaptation changes is:

### Stage A — Build the real-world entity graph

For true-story/biographical films, create structured source profiles for:

- names and aliases
- family/community background where publicly relevant
- religion/caste/community where explicitly documented and materially relevant
- spouse/family identities
- education and profession
- political/social affiliations
- marriage type and major life events
- places and chronology
- public statements and memoir claims

Use primary or high-quality sources wherever possible.

### Stage B — Build the film entity graph

From subtitles, transcripts, scene descriptions and visual analysis, record:

- character names
- stated identities
- explicit religious/caste markers
- symbols and portraits
- costume cues when narratively meaningful
- dialogue about ideology/community
- wedding/funeral/ritual scenes
- hero/villain/moral-authority roles

### Stage C — Compute adaptation deltas

Compare source-person graph against film-character graph and surface:

- identity omitted
- identity added
- identity swapped
- ideological affiliation inserted
- historical event removed or inverted
- community marker transferred from one character to another
- invented stereotype or caricature

### Stage D — Scene verification

Where legally available, use timed subtitles/transcripts and frame-level review to verify the lead. The output should cite the exact scene or time window whenever possible.

### Stage E — Independent corroboration

Check primary records, memoirs, interviews, official biographies, contemporaneous reporting and creator explanations. Reviews are witnesses to claims, not final judges.

### Stage F — Adjudication

Assign:

- `VERIFIED`
- `SUPPORTED`
- `DISPUTED`
- `UNVERIFIED`
- `FALSE / CONTRADICTED`

and separately score confidence for Fact, Interpretation and Intent.

## 9. Worked discovery pattern: Soorarai Pottru

A social-media allegation that the film changed G. R. Gopinath's social/ideological identity is exactly the kind of claim this pipeline should discover.

Independent corroborating material currently establishes that:

- Gopinath's full name was Gorur Ramaswamy Iyengar Gopinath and reliable profiles identify his Tamil Iyengar family background.
- Contemporary coverage of *Soorarai Pottru* explicitly said the film fictionalized Gopinath's autobiography by placing the protagonist in a different social setting.
- Later mainstream commentary identifies the real-life traditional Brahmin wedding as having been rewritten as a self-respect marriage with Periyar/Ambedkar imagery.
- Regional reporting describes the fictional Maara as a Periyarist.

This supports a **verified adaptation-delta investigation**. It does not by itself establish the filmmaker's subjective intent. The proper finding is therefore structured rather than rhetorical:

```text
REAL-WORLD IDENTITY: documented Iyengar background
FILM SOCIAL SETTING: materially changed
IDEOLOGICAL INSERTION: Periyar/self-respect symbolism supported by film commentary
FACT CONFIDENCE: high
INTERPRETATION: ideological substitution / appropriation concern supported
INTENT: requires separate evidence
```

## 10. Editorial guardrails

- Criticism of one Hindu/Brahmin/Muslim/Christian/etc. character is not automatically community hatred.
- Caste criticism is not automatically anti-Hindu.
- Opposition to tyranny or inequality is not automatically Left/progressive.
- National-security framing is not required to present a rival state's narrative as equally valid for certification purposes.
- Regional folk traditions must be understood locally before being mapped to pan-Indian categories.
- A source's ideological orientation should be recorded when relevant, but claims are adjudicated through evidence rather than excluded because of viewpoint.
- Professional reviews, activist commentary and partisan commentary may all surface valid factual leads; none receives automatic authority on interpretation.

## 11. Public output model

For v0.x, avoid false precision from a 0–100 Sanghi score.

Public result should contain:

- **Certified / Friendly / Mixed / Not Certified / Unrated**
- dimension profile
- confidence
- concise reasons
- Narrative Integrity flags
- verified/disputed evidence notes

Example:

```text
SANGHI CERTIFIED
Confidence: High

Rashtra: Strong
Raksha: Strong
Dharma: Moderate

Why:
- Indian sovereignty and national security form the film's moral frame.
- Armed-forces/intelligence service is treated positively.

Narrative Integrity:
- 1 factuality dispute
- no certification penalty merely for presenting India's sovereignty position
```

## 12. Next validation milestone

Create a 30-film gold set:

- 10 obvious Certified
- 10 obvious Not Certified
- 10 difficult/contested

For the difficult set, require at least one scene-level or adaptation-delta audit before final adjudication.

The gold set becomes the regression suite for future classifier changes.
