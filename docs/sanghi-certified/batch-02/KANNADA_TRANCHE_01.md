(2) Those sacred archetypes are mapped onto violent gangsters; the director describes the work as anti-violence, but the sacred/criminal fusion is still a real interpretive tension rather than straightforward devotional affirmation.

## Tranche result

10 researched; 10 gate-passed; 0 provisional. Distribution: Certified 1; Mixed / Contested 4; Neutral 5; Not Certified 0.

### Rubric observations

- Sacred metaphor is not automatically Sacred Regard 5/5: GGVV and Toby show why transposition into violent/criminal archetypes should be described rather than flattened.
- Social-harmony stories require representation-asymmetry review without turning majority-group criticism into an automatic anti-Indic finding; Daredevil Musthafa is the regression case.
- Filial/family dharma can create a real certification signal without explicit temple/deity content; Godhi Banna tests this dimension.
- Strong regional texture alone is insufficient for certification; Hostel Hudugaru, Lucia and KGF Chapter 1 remain Neutral controls.
- Caste critique remains non-disqualifying by default; Kaatera requires evidence of identity weaponization or sacred contempt before moving toward Not Certified.
