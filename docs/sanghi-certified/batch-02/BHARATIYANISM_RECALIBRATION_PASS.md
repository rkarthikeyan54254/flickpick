## Methodology lessons locked by this pass

- **Bharatiya alignment ≠ factual infallibility.** Certification and Narrative Integrity are separate outputs.
- **No performative centrism.** The classifier can identify India-first, Hindu-civilizational, temple/heritage, anti-coercion and national-security signals positively when supported.
- **No evidence discount.** A Bharatiya concern being real does not authenticate a specific number, conspiracy scale, motive, or community-wide generalization.
- **No inverse bias.** Caste reform, female agency, queer characters, police criticism, state criticism, or social injustice do not automatically count against Bharatiya alignment.
- **No false symmetry.** A sourced problem does not need an unrelated counterexample from another group to be reportable.
- **Narrative Integrity travels with the badge.** High-severity warnings must remain prominent even when a title is Certified.
- **Neutral must be earned.** It means an adversarial pass found no material Bharatiya/civilizational signal strong enough to classify otherwise.
